03 / ARCHITECT

JAVIER
SENOSIAIN

Javier Senosiain is a Mexican architect known for organic architecture. His buildings often use curves and natural forms instead of traditional straight lines. Nautilus House is one of his best-known projects.

08 / 01
SOCIAL SPACE

LIVING
ROOM

The living room grows from the interior garden. Instead of normal walls, the space uses curves and built-in furniture. The room feels open and connected to nature.

09 / 02
ENTERTAINMENT

TV
ROOM

The TV room is placed inside the large belly of the shell. The seats follow the curved walls and create one continuous space.

10 / SERVICE SPACE

THE
KITCHEN.

The kitchen is also part of the organic design. The cabinets follow the curved wall and the surfaces are integrated into the structure. Nothing feels like a separate box.

11 / 03
PRIVATE SPACE

BED
ROOM

The bedroom continues the same soft and curved design. The bed and storage follow the shape of the walls. There are no hard rectangular corners.

12 / 04
PRIVATE SPACE

BATH
ROOM

The bathroom looks like a small natural cave. It has curved surfaces, blue mosaic details and a round opening for daylight.

13 / 05
PRIVATE SPACE

THE
STUDY

The study is a quiet space inside the shell. The curved desk and shelves are built into the walls. A special window gives a view toward the landscape.
